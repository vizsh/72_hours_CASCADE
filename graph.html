How a link earns its line.

For every facility, the twin regresses what it observed against what its upstream neighbours were doing one lag earlier, after removing the direct effect of the hazard. Each candidate link gets a zero-mean prior, so nothing is assumed. Its prior precision is re-estimated as evidence arrives (automatic relevance determination). A link whose upstream never left its normal band carries no information and stays a candidate; an exercised link is confirmed or pruned.

Seven of Nagpur's 102 candidate links were planted as fakes: plausible on paper, absent in the hidden world. Tick Reveal planted fakes to see whether the graph was fooled. The learned weights are the ones the operations room uses to explain every forecast.
